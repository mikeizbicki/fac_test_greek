# Art Style Instructions

The artwork should embrace a warm, storybook illustration style reminiscent of classic children's literature with Mediterranean influences. Use soft, muted earth tones—olive greens, warm ochres, dusty blues, and weathered stone grays—to evoke ancient Greek settings. Characters should have gentle, rounded features with expressive eyes and welcoming faces that appeal to young children. The style should blend realistic proportions with slight cartoon simplification for accessibility. Employ traditional media aesthetics like watercolor washes or colored pencil textures rather than sharp digital rendering. Backgrounds should suggest ancient Greek architecture and landscapes through simple, iconic elements—olive trees, stone paths, terracotta roofs, and distant hills—without overwhelming detail. The overall mood should be cozy and inviting, with warm lighting that creates a sense of safety and wonder appropriate for bedtime stories or quiet learning moments.
Never draw text.

Always fill the full picture with content.
