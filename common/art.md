# Art Style Guide

Overall look: hyperrealistic, high-key 3D animation with bright, saturated color and crisp readability. Use PBR materials with simplified micro-surface detail to avoid grit (5–10% roughness variation max), gentle subsurface scattering for skin, and clean, glossy highlights; textures should be clean and graphic rather than photogrungy. Light with a warm key (4500–5200K), cool fill (7000–8000K), and a defined rim; supplement with a neutral HDRI for soft global bounce. Keep shadows soft (larger light radius), AO light to moderate to prevent murk, and exposure slightly above mid-gray for a luminous midtone bias; add subtle bloom (5–10%) and a mild filmic curve. Favor wide-to-normal lenses (24–50mm), dynamic but readable compositions, and room for on-screen gags. Depth of field shallow but not extreme (f/2.8–4 feel), motion blur low (120–180° shutter), and optional 1–2 px toon edge/rim outlines for clarity. Render at 2K+ with minimal denoise to keep edges sharp.

Cartoon violence/gags are deliberately flat 2D overlays contrasted against the hyperreal base. Composite them in screen space with no lighting, shading, perspective warp, drop shadows, or contact shadows—keep them hard-edged and untextured. Daze stars: flat five-point shapes in canary yellow (#FFE23A) orbiting the head on a perfect circle, 6–10 frames per revolution with slight scale ping-pong; X-eyes: white Xs with a 2–3 px black stroke replacing pupils for 6–12 frames; sick tint: a lime green (#52E06B) lower-face wash at 60–75% opacity, optionally with faint vertical lines. Pancake smashes: compress along the vertical axis to 10–20% of height with lateral widening, hold 1–2 frames, then elastic rebound with 15–25% overshoot and 2–3 oscillations; add 1–3 smear frames for fast actions and a single white impact flash plus a thin radial ring. Timing is snappy: fast in, 1–2 frame holds on extremes, quick recoveries; injuries reset immediately, no gore or damage detail. Remove gags cleanly between beats to restore the glossy, high-saturation realism, maintaining the intentional clash between flat 2D effects and polished 3D forms.
