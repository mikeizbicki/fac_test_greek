# About This Series

The purpose is to teach Koine Greek to children through *fun* stories.
The stories also teach about ancient Greek culture (600BC - 200AD) and common Biblical lessons.

## Target Audience

Children ages 4–10, with no prior Greek required.

## Features

Vocabulary:
- Simple, concrete vocabulary useful for children in everyday lives.
- Focus on high-frequency Koine words found in the New Testament.
- Focus on words that have cognates in English or Spanish (so that they are easier to learn and children can understand more about their own native languages).
- The meaning of new words should be understandable from context, so that learning does not require memorization.

Grammar:
- Key patterns are repeated many times in the same story with different vocabulary.
  (This ensures that children can absorb the meaning of the grammar without memorization.)
- Key patterns are reused from previous stories to reinforce those lessons.
