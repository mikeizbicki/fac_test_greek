# Glaux (Owl) — Artist Instructions

Glaux is a small, round-bodied owl with large, round amber eyes that have dark brown pupils, set within a pale cream-colored facial disk. The edges of her facial disk are light brown, and her head is smoothly rounded without ear tufts. Her feathers are mottled in warm medium brown and light cream: her back and wings are medium brown with darker umber speckles, while her chest and belly are light cream with soft brown flecks. She has a small pale tan beak and yellow-ochre colored feet with charcoal-gray talons. Her tail is short and matches the brown tones of her back.
