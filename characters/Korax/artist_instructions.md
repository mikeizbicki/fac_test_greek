# Korax — Artist Instructions

Korax is a male crow with glossy black plumage that shows cool blue‑violet iridescent highlights on the head, shoulders, wing coverts, and tail in light, but with a few matte charcoal scuffs that keep his feathers a little scruffy. He has a strong, straight black beak with a tiny visible chip near the tip and faint scuffing along the edges; his wedge‑shaped tail always shows a small notch in one outer feather. His very dark (near‑black) eyes always include a crisp white catch‑light under a subtly heavy brow line, and a short fringe of raised neck hackles gives a rough, tousled outline at the throat and nape. His legs and feet are black and scaly with sturdy black claws that run a touch long, and keep his size consistent: when perched on a low post he stands about knee‑high to a young child, with long primaries and a compact, slightly hunched body.

Korax always looks angry and unkempt.
