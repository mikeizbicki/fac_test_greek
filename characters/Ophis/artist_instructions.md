# Ophis — Artist Instructions

Ophis is a long, slender, limbless serpent with dark-olive scales that carry a muted bronze sheen, and a few slightly rough, uneven scale edges that make him look a touch scruffy. A subtle Greek meander pattern in bronze runs continuously along the center of his back from head to tail. His half-lidded golden eyes sit a bit deep with dark moss-olive rings, and their narrow vertical pupils widen in warm light. His mouth tilts into a faintly crooked, too-wide smile; a bright red, forked tongue often flickers, and one slightly overlong, pale-ivory fang always shows and catches the light.
