Voice: warm, moonlit, grandmotherly contralto with feather‑soft authority and twinkling kindness—overflowing with jubilant relief and protective pride. Give a delighted, breath‑lifted gasp on “ἰδοὺ!” with an audible smile; keep the tempo lively and contained to about five seconds, riding a gentle crescendo of hopeful excitement. Enunciate the Greek clearly and musically—round, tender consonants; savor “ὁ ὑπερβρέφος” with awe and affection—and land “ἔρχεται!” in a triumphant yet comforting assurance (never sharp). Let the pitch rise as she points, then settle warmly on the final word; a soft, joyful breath or tiny chuckle of relief is welcome to heighten the sense that help has arrived.
