Silky low baritone that purrs with controlled, polite menace—let the hunger be unmistakable and intimate, as if addressing frightened children you fully intend to devour. Deliver slowly and deliberately, with crystal-clear consonants and stretched, savoring vowels; loom with the rhythm, never raising volume. Pounce lightly but precisely on “νῦν,” “βούλομαι,” and especially “ἐσθίειν,” giving a tiny tongue-click of consideration before the final word and a brief, savoring hum on it, like you’re tasting the idea. Let a faint, sibilant chuckle or smiling breath leak at the end, making the threat feel inevitable rather than loud.
