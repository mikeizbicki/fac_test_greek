A silky, low baritone that purrs with restrained hunger—courteous, controlled, and calculating, a playful menace smiling through the teeth. Deliver the line in a slow, prowling rhythm with micro-pauses, never raising volume; pounce precisely on νῦν and especially ἐσθίειν, stretching vowels as if tasting them and keeping consonants crisp. Begin with a quiet savoring hum, let a thin, polite smile color the words, and finish with either a soft sibilant chuckle or a delicate tongue-click, as though deciding which bite to take first. Make the threat sound like a gracious promise—intimate, chilling, and inevitable—aimed off-left toward the children, with no rush over five seconds.
