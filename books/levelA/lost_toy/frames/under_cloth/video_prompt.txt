Begin with the Dinosaur kneeling stage-right by the bed in the family house, lifting the cloth with one paw and peeking underneath. For about 2 seconds he leans in and scans left-to-right under the cloth, eyes darting as he searches (ζητεῖ ὑπὸ τοῦ ἱματίου). He then gives a small head shake, lets the cloth fall back down to the floor, and looks a little puzzled—no toy visible—ending still stage-right facing the bed (οὐχ εὑρίσκει).
