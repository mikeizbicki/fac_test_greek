Start with a medium-wide, low eye-level view of the agora portico and lawn. Ophis slithers in from the bottom-right edge in slow S-curves, his bronze-olive scales gleaming and tongue flicking curiously, while the Panda steps up toward the wooden door looking calm. The camera gently dollies in and slightly pans left to keep both characters framed as Ophis glides toward the red ball and the Panda pushes the door open. Panda, still calm, slips through the doorway into the building and the door swings mostly closed; Ophis pauses beside the red ball, lifts his head, and smiles slyly. End on a steady medium shot with Ophis coiled on the right next to the red ball and the closed door behind; keep both Ophis and Panda in the shot (at least from the knees up) until the Panda passes through the doorway.
