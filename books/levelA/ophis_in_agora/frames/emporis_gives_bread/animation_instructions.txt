Begin with a medium‑wide view down the agora colonnade; keep Emporis, Emporos, Panda, Dinosaur, and Ophis in the shot from at least the knees up at all times. Emporis enters from the left carrying two small round loaves with golden crusts as the camera gently pans left-to-center to follow her approach to the children. Panda smiles and reaches out with both hands to take one loaf, while Dinosaur eagerly extends his arms for the other; Emporos stands just behind them, watching approvingly with a warm smile. The camera dollies in slightly as Emporis, cheerful, places a loaf into each child’s hands. As the giving finishes, Ophis, calm and content, begins to slither toward the colonnade opening on the right to exit while the camera eases a touch right to keep everyone visible; end with Panda and Dinosaur each holding a loaf, Emporis beside them with empty hands, Emporos smiling behind them, and Ophis at the right edge mid‑body visible near the column.
