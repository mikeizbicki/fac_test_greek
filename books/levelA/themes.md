# LevelA

This is the first level of the series.
The purpose is to gently build the child's movement/location vocabulary so that future levels can tell more interesting stories and introduce more interesting grammar.

## Grammar

- Use only present tense verbs in the 3rd person (singular or plural).
    - Both active and middle tense is okay (but no passive).
    - Do NOT use: commands, participles, past/future tense.
- All nouns should be written with an article to help students learn their cases.
    - All cases are allowed.
    - Singular and plural forms are allowed.
- Sentence structure should match English sentence structure as much as possible.
- The subjects and objects of nouns should always be written and never implied.

## Vocabulary

### Nouns

Household objects:
- ἡ κλίνη
- ἡ τράπεζα
- ἡ κιβωτός
- ὁ σάκκος
- ἡ θύρα
- ἡ θυρίς
- τὸ μῆλον

Food/drink:
- ὁ ἄρτος (bread)
- τὸ ὕδωρ (water)

Objects to hold / play with:
- τὸ βιβλίον
- ἡ σφαῖρα
- ὁ κύβος
- ὁ λίθος

- ἔρχομαι (and compounds like: διέρχομαι, εἰσέρχομαι, ἐξέρχομαι, προσέρχομαι, περιέρχομαι, συνέρχομαι)

- φέρω (and compounds like: εἰσφέρω, ἐκφέρω, καταφέρω, προσφέρω)
- πίπτω (and compounds like: καταπίπτω)
- τρέχω
- μένω
- πέτομαι
