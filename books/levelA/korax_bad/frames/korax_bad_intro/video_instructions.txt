Korax swoops in from off-camera left into the sky, beating his wings and popping slightly toward camera on “ἰδού!” as the clouds drift slowly. He hovers midair with a scowling face, opens his beak in an angry caw timed to “ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ ὁ Κόραξ ἐστίν,” then flaps harder with a sharp, jerky bob to show “μάλα κακός.” He finishes by arcing to stage-right and holds a hover there, still glaring with wings half-spread.
