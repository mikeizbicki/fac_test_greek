In the schoolyard, Panda sits on the ground by the blue doors, smiling and reading a small leather‑bound book with bronze clasps.  
Korax, a sleek black crow, enters from above right, swooping down with wings spread wide and an angry, intent look.  
Panda notices the rush, his smile vanishes; he gasps, eyes wide, and lifts one paw toward the air.  
Korax sweeps past in side view and snatches the book with his beak.  
Panda leans back on the dirt, looking shocked and worried, reaching toward the book.  
Korax beats his wings hard while gripping the book in his beak, feathers ruffled with mischievous energy.
