- Outside the school, Panda sits on the ground and looks up happily at the Didaskalos, who smiles and holds a small leather-bound book with bronze clasps.
- The Didaskalos gently offers the book, and Panda takes it with both paws, sitting upright with a bright, joyful face.
- Panda opens the parchment pages and begins to read, eyes moving down the lines as his mouth murmurs softly with delight.
- The Didaskalos gives an encouraging nod, steps back, and exits stage right past the porch.
- Panda keeps reading with a big, happy grin, occasionally bouncing his shoulders with excitement as he follows the text.
- Vines, leaves, and the empty swing sway lightly in a calm breeze while Panda continues to read joyfully.
