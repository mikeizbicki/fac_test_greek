Outside the school, Panda sits on the ground near the left side, with the swing and slide nearby. 
He looks downward, and his face is sad. 
In the first second, his head dips a little more and his shoulders droop. 
He slowly shifts his front paws a bit closer together on the ground. 
He blinks once, eyes heavy, still looking down. 
The vines by the door and the tree leaves sway gently as Panda breathes softly, sad and still.
