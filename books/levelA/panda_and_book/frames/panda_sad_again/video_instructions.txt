- In the schoolyard, Panda sits on the ground near the swing and slide, looking up in shock with one paw raised as Korax the crow flies past holding the brown book in his beak.
- Korax beats his wings and continues flying left over the swing, carrying the book away.
- Korax exits stage left with the book, out of the scene.
- Panda lowers his raised paw, his shoulders droop, and worry turns to sadness.
- He looks down at his empty paws and sniffles, feeling upset.
- Tears well and then stream down Panda’s cheeks as he sits quietly, clearly sad.
