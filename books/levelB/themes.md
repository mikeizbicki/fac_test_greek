# LevelB

This level introduces infinitive verbs and expands into outdoor adventures and classic stories.
The purpose is to teach children how to express wants, abilities, and intentions while exploring exciting new settings.

## Grammar

- Continue using present tense verbs in 3rd person (singular/plural).
- **NEW**: Introduce infinitive verbs with main verbs that take infinitive complements.
- All nouns still written with articles.
- All cases allowed, with more emphasis on accusative objects.
- Subjects and objects may be implied if they are very obvious.

## Vocabulary

### Verbs

Verbs to use with infinitives:
- μανθάνω (I learn)
- διδάσκω (I teach) 
- φοβέομαι (I fear, I am afraid)
- μέλλω (I am about to, I intend)
- ἄρχομαι (I begin)
- παύομαι (I stop, I cease)
- δεῖ (it is necessary - impersonal)
- δύναμαι (I am able, I can)
- βούλομαι (I want, I wish)
- θέλω (I want, I desire)

Basic needs:
- πεινάω (I am hungry) - should always be used with βούλομαι φαγεῖν or θέλω φαγεῖν to help illustrate the definitions
- διψάω (I am thirsty)

Adventure:
- μάχομαι (I fight)
- πολεμέω (I make war)
- παίω (I strike, I hit)
- τύπτω (I strike, I hit)
- σῴζω (I save, I rescue)
- οἰκοδομέω (I build, I construct)
- καταστρέφω (I destroy)

Trade verbs:
- πωλέω (I sell)
- ἀγοράζω (I buy)
- ἀνοίγω (open)
- κλείω (close)

### Nouns

Locations:
- ὁ δρυμός (forest)
- τὸ ὄρος (mountain)
- ἡ ἔρημος (desert)
- ἡ θάλασσα (sea, ocean)
- ὁ ποταμός (river)
- ἡ λίμνη (lake)
- ἡ ὁδός (road, path)

Ground/terrain:
- ἡ γῆ (ground, dirt)
- ὁ χόρτος (grass)
- ἡ ἄμμος (sand)
- τὸ ξύλον (wood)

Structures:
- ἡ σκηνή (tent)
- τὸ τεῖχος (wall)
- ὁ πύργος (tower)

Animals:
- τὸ πρόβατον (sheep)
- ὁ ἵππος (horse)
- ὁ κύων (dog)

Money/materials:
- ὁ χρυσός (gold)
- ὁ ἄργυρος (silver)
- ὁ χαλκός (bronze)
- τὸ νόμισμα (coin)
- ἡ δραχμή (drachma)

Weapons:
- ἡ μάχαιρα (sword)
- τὸ τόξον (bow)
- ὁ βέλος (arrow/projectile)
- τό δόρυ (spear)
- ἡ ἀσπίς (shield)
