# Dinosaur Buys Bread

Dinosaur is in the house.
Dinosaur is hungry (he wants to eat bread).
Dinosaur looks for bread, but there is no bread.
Dinosaur needs to buy bread. He needs to go to the agora.
He finds some coins on the table and grabs them and puts them in his sack.
Dinosaur leaves the house. (taking the sack with him)
Dinosaur walks to the agora (location is both house exterior and agora exterior)
Dinosaur enters agora.
Dinosaur walks to emporos.
Emporos has bread and apples and water for sale.
Dinosaur reaches into his sack and takes out the coins.
Dinosaur gives emporos coins and buys bread.
Emporos gives dinosaur bread.
Dinosaur eats bread. Now he is not hungry.
