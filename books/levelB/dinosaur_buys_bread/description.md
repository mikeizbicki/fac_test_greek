# Dinosaur Buys Bread

Frame 1: Dinosaur is in the house.
Frame 2: Dinosaur is hungry (he wants to eat bread).
Frame 3: Dinosaur looks for bread, but there is no bread.
Frame 4: Dinosaur needs to buy bread. He needs to go to the agora.
Frame 5: Dinosaur leaves the house.
Frame 6: Dinosaur walks to the agora (location is both house exterior and agora exterior)
Frame 7: Dinosaur enters agora.
Frame 8: Dinosaur walks to emporos.
Frame 9: Emporos has bread and apples and water.
Frame 10: Dinosaur gives emporos coins.
Frame 11: Emporos gives dinosaur bread.
Frame 12: Dinosaur eats bread. Now he is not hungry.
