The Dinosaur, starting stage-right beside the chair at center, crouches and pokes his snout under the seat as he “ζητεῖ ὑπὸ τῆς καθέδρας.”  
At the 2-second mark, the red ball rolls out from under the chair to the front of stage-left and comes to a stop.  
He perks up with a big smile, steps toward the ball, and taps it happily; final positions: Dinosaur stage-right near the chair, ball front stage-left, chair centered.
